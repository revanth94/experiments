## How to Access Official Reports

1. Visit: https://ntca.gov.in
2. Navigate to: **Publications** or **Resources**
3. Download: "All India Tiger Estimation" reports (PDF)

Alternative: Ministry of Environment website (https://moef.gov.in)

## Data Confidence

- ✅ **National totals**: Official government census data
- ✅ **State breakdowns**: From NTCA reports
- ✅ **Year-over-year**: Directly comparable methodology
- ⚠️ **Confidence intervals**: Point estimates used (actual ranges ±5-10%)
