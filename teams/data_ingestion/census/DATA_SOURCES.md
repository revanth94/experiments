# Data Sources - India Tiger Census

## Primary Data Source

### National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA)
- **Organization**: Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change
- **Official Website**: https://ntca.gov.in
- **Authority**: Official body responsible for tiger conservation in India under Project Tiger
- **Established**: 2006 (under Wildlife Protection Act)

### Census Methodology
- **Survey Method**: Camera trap surveys with capture-recapture statistical models
- **Frequency**: Every 4 years (2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022)
- **Coverage**: All tiger-occupied areas across 18 states
- **Technology**: ~26,000+ camera traps deployed in 2022 census
- **Statistical Model**: Spatially explicit capture-recapture (SECR) models

## Official Reports

### 2022 Census
- **Report Title**: "Status of Tigers, Co-predators and Prey in India 2022"
- **Release Date**: April 9, 2023 (50th anniversary of Project Tiger)
- **Total Tigers**: 3,682
- **Geographic Coverage**: 53 tiger reserves across 18 states
- **Survey Period**: 2021-2022

### 2018 Census
- **Report Title**: "Status of Tigers, Co-predators and Prey in India 2018"
- **Release Date**: July 29, 2019
- **Total Tigers**: 2,967
- **Notable**: 26,838 camera trap locations, 34.8 million photographs analyzed

### 2014 Census
- **Report Title**: "Status of Tigers, Co-predators and Prey in India 2014"
- **Release Date**: January 20, 2015
- **Total Tigers**: 2,226

### 2010 Census
- **Report Title**: "Status of Tigers in India 2010"
- **Release Date**: March 28, 2011
- **Total Tigers**: 1,706
- **Notable**: First use of camera trap methodology at national scale

### 2006 Census
- **Report Title**: "Status of Tigers in India 2006"
- **Release Date**: 2008
- **Total Tigers**: 1,411
- **Notable**: Conducted after 2005 crisis when population had declined significantly

## Secondary Sources (Verification & Context)

### International Organizations

#### World Wildlife Fund (WWF)
- **Website**: https://www.worldwildlife.org
- **Role**: Global tiger conservation partner, tracks TX2 goal (double tigers by 2022)
- **Data**: Global tiger population trends, cross-country comparisons
- **Key Report**: "Tiger population update 2016" - documented first global increase in 100 years

#### International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN)
- **Website**: https://www.iucnredlist.org
- **Data**: Tiger conservation status (Endangered)
- **Assessment**: Population trends, threats, conservation measures
- **Updates**: Regular assessments with peer-reviewed data

#### Global Tiger Forum (GTF)
- **Type**: Intergovernmental organization (13 tiger-range countries)
- **Website**: https://globaltigerforum.org
- **Data**: International tiger population estimates
- **2025 Estimate**: 5,574 wild tigers globally

## Data Limitations & Caveats

### Known Limitations
1. **Census Frequency**: Data only available every 4 years; intervening years are estimates
2. **Confidence Intervals**: NTCA reports include statistical confidence intervals (not included in simplified dataset)
3. **Tiger Movement**: Individual tigers may move between states/reserves between censuses
4. **Survey Coverage**: Some remote/conflict areas may have incomplete coverage
5. **Subspecies**: Data does not distinguish Bengal tiger subspecies variations

### Estimation Uncertainty
- Each census has an associated confidence interval (typically ±5-10%)
- Example: 2022 count of 3,682 has confidence range of ~3,167-4,031 (official NTCA report)
- Simplified dataset uses point estimates for clarity
