For this step, we need to ask the customer a series of questions to understand their requirements.

We need to collect the following pieces of information:
1. **Customer name** (optional)
2. **Location of interest** (required) - Specific geographic area to be analyzed
3. **Ecological impacts** (required) - Types of environmental factors to assess
4. **Purpose of analysis** (required) - Why they need this analysis
5. **Report types and deliverables** (required) - What outputs they need (e.g., executive summary, technical report, GIS maps, EMP)
6. **Additional information** (optional) - Timeline, historical data needs, specific constraints
